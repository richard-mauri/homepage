## November 13, 2026 — temporary authenticated guest checkpoint

- **Scope:** Existing Homepage draft PR #33 and ScraperGo draft PR #133 only. No merges, releases, service restarts, token changes, or sister-app changes.
- **Homepage:** Added `POST /api/session/guest` issuing a cryptographically random one-hour, `guest-xxx` authenticated identity. Guest session and child-handoff maps are **process memory only**, never in the durable token/session store. The five-minute garbage collector and opportunistic checks delete expired guest records. Restart immediately invalidates guests. Issuance does not extend a live guest's expiry.
- **Policy:** A guest may use public app functionality through the existing authenticated child-handoff/profile protocol. Homepage denies guest server settings persistence even with a guest child bearer or guest cookie. No guest saved preferences or automatic restore. A named user remains separately authenticated with their own personal credential and settings.
- **ScraperGo:** The reader begins disabled until its Homepage-issued identity validates. When no valid identity is found, a modal explains Homepage sign-in or the one-hour guest option and offers a user-initiated link. Validation happens in place; there is **no automatic navigation**. A validated guest is shown as `Signed in as guest-xxx`, using the same app identity mechanism as a named user.
- **Acceptance required:** Verify Apple Silicon Homepage with `./homego.sh` and ScraperGo with its existing ARM64 launcher; never run `darwin-amd64` on the owner Mac. Check named user and temporary guest in Safari, five-minute idle heartbeat, expiry after one hour, no automatic navigation, settings denial, and no interference with other suite services. Exact-head CI green is required before owner validation. Do not merge before owner approval.
