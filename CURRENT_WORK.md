- **Owner request:** The Users & Access panel shows active `guest-xxx` identities until their one-hour expiration, but administrators have no direct way to disconnect an obsolete guest.
- **Implementation:** Added a confirmed **Revoke guest** button to each temporary guest entry. Other guests, named users and approved credentials are unaffected; no guest data is written to disk.
